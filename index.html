Definitions & assumptions used in this prototype
  • Budgets are explicit monthly amounts, never prorated from an annual figure. The org budget is its own number; team allocations are a subset of it, and the remainder is held centrally. Unallocated spend (no team mapping) counts toward the org budget and has no team budget line. Remaining = monthly budget − spend to date.
  • Spend labels: Actual = billed or expense-matched money; Estimated = usage × list price before invoice, and all month-end figures (spend to date × days in month ÷ days elapsed — a straight run-rate, not a forecasting model); Inferred = deduced from browser/network signals. Inferred activity is never shown as spend.
  • Active users (per app) = unique identities observed using that app in the period. Organization-level counts deduplicate mapped employees across apps; accounts that can’t be matched to a person are shown separately and never added to employee counts.
  • Approval status (Approved / Unreviewed / Not approved) is a governance decision. Risk class (Low / Medium / High / Not assessed) is an independent assessment — an approved app can be high risk, and an unreviewed app can be low risk.
  • Limits are org-wide per application. USD, token and credit/request limits use different units and reset periods, so they are shown in separate groups and never ranked against each other.
  • Selecting a team scopes spend, usage and the table to that team. The team list keeps every team for comparison and highlights the selection. Limits stay company-wide; the team’s share is overlaid.
